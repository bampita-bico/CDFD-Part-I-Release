## Summary

This release ships the **honesty / correction revision** of the Part I fundamental-physics archive: twelve article-class manuscripts with tightened claim language, an explicit correction ledger, and demotion of “named laws” packaging to postulated CDFT notation and falsifiable targets.

Part I remains an **open preprint archive**. Supplementary scripts reproduce internal calculations and consistency checks. They do **not** establish empirical validation of the physical hypotheses.
