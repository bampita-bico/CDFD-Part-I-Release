# Methods Paper Readiness

**Current decision:** do not submit.

## Completed locally

- A versioned JSON Schema declares scientific context, baseline, claim
  boundary, calibration status, audit, falsification, and provenance.
- A completed negative-result example demonstrates that a failed criterion is
  preserved rather than converted into a fitted success.
- The archive-to-publication consolidation register identifies what must not be
  represented as a validated scientific result.

## Required external evaluation

1. Recruit at least two researchers who were not involved in CDFD to annotate
   the same set of model reports using the record. Measure agreement on claim
   type, calibration status, and whether a native baseline is present.
2. Compare structured records with the original free-form reports for the time
   needed to identify missing provenance, non-finite output, post-hoc fitting,
   or an unsupported claim.
3. Publish all materials, analysis code, failures, and feedback. A positive
   usability finding is not enough: the protocol must show that it improves a
   concrete review or reproducibility task.
4. Have a domain expert review the relationship to existing standards before
   claiming novelty or submitting a methods paper.

## Rejection criterion

Do not publish this as a new standard if reviewers find that existing model,
simulation, and research-object standards plus a conventional preregistration
template already cover the useful function, or if the schema does not improve
reviewability on the pre-specified tasks.
