# Consolidation Register

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Purpose:** prevent the CDFD/AFL archive from being represented as dozens of
independent discoveries.

| Material | Archival status | Publication disposition | Required evidence before any submission |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part I physics | Retain with its binding correction notice. | Do not pursue a broad theory paper. Use only the finite-thickness non-result or a separately verified algebra note. | Independent mathematical priority/proof or independently reviewed numerical methods. |
| Part II origins-of-life papers | Retain as corrected toy-model and teaching material. | Do not submit as an AFL discovery series. | A domain-native empirical or chemical study with named controls. |
| Part III biomedical papers | Retain as corrected hypotheses and proposal sources. | Do not submit the reframe papers as clinical discoveries. | Ethics-approved, preregistered study with field-native baseline and independent data. |
| Part IV domain series | Retain as corrected exploratory material. | Do not submit individually as universal-law papers. | A concrete application that improves a named native comparator on held-out data. |
| This methods directory | New consolidated spine. | One modest methods paper after external evaluation. | Independent users, audited records, and evidence that the protocol improves reviewability. |
| CDFD Runtime | Separate research-software route. | One software paper, not evidence for the framework. | Versioned archive DOI, public release history, external use or a compelling reproducible research workflow comparison. |

No archive file is deleted or silently relabeled. The correction notices remain
the authoritative record for prior content. "Consolidation" means reducing what
is presented for publication, not concealing what was previously written.
