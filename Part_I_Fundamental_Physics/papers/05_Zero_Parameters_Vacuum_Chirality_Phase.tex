\begin{abstract}
Paper~IV of this series derived the master self-consistency equation for the
CDFT vacuum density $\rho_0$ and identified the vacuum chirality phase
$\phi_c$ as the single remaining unknown.
The present paper derives $\phi_c$ from the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ algebraic structure
of the trefoil vortex itself, closing the loop.

The argument is a self-consistency condition:
the Brannen $\mathbb{Z}_3$ parametrisation of the lepton masses has a
universal invariant $Q = 2/3$ (the Koide ratio, proved in Paper~II).
The chirality equilibrium condition (Paper~III) requires $3\theta + \phi_c = \pi$.
Self-consistency demands that the chirality breaking angle $3\theta$
equals the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ invariant $Q$:
\begin{equation*}
  3\theta \;=\; Q \;=\; \tfrac{2}{3}\,,
  \quad \Rightarrow \quad
  \phi_c \;=\; \pi - \tfrac{2}{3}\,.
\end{equation*}
This gives $\theta = 2/9$~rad exactly.

With one input ($m_e$ as the mass unit), the theory predicts:
\begin{align*}
  m_\mu  &= 105.659~\text{MeV} \quad (+9.8~\text{ppm from PDG 2022}) \\
  m_\tau &= 1776.985~\text{MeV} \quad (+0.125~\text{MeV} = +1.04\,\sigma\text{ from PDG 2022})
\end{align*}
The Koide formula $Q = 2/3$ holds exactly within the parametrisation.
The CDFT lepton-sector construction is therefore internally closed, with
$m_e$ as the external mass-unit input and no additional fitted parameter in
this step.

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The Constraint-Driven Field Theory (CDFT) series has, paper by paper,
derived the structure of the lepton sector from the geometry of the vacuum medium:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Paper~I}~\cite{MujjabiI2026}: $\alpha = 1/137.036$ from vortex ring
    equilibrium.
  \item \textbf{Paper~II}~\cite{MujjabiII2026}: Koide formula $Q = 2/3$ as a
    theorem of $\mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry; leptons as trefoil $T(2,3)$ modes.
  \item \textbf{Paper~III}~\cite{MujjabiIII2026}: All four open problems closed;
    $\rho_0$ identified as the sole remaining unknown.
    Chirality equilibrium: $3\theta + \phi_c = \pi$.
  \item \textbf{Paper~IV}~\cite{MujjabiIV2026}: Master self-consistency equation
    for $\rho_0$; Compton--ring coincidence; $M_5 = 460.4$~MeV prediction.
\end{enumerate}

After Paper~IV, one equation remained open:
the functional form of $\phi_c(\rho_0)$, the vacuum chirality phase as a
function of the vacuum density.

The present paper resolves this.
We show that $\phi_c$ is not an independent vacuum property but is fixed by
the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ algebraic structure of the trefoil itself:
$\phi_c = \pi - 2/3$.
This gives $\theta = 2/9$~rad, predicts $m_\mu$ and $m_\tau$ from $m_e$ alone,
and closes the CDFT lepton sector.

\subsection{Equilibrium memory is not memory absence}

The zero-parameter closure uses the equilibrium limit of the adaptive ratio:
$S\cdot M_s\rightarrow1$. This should not be read as a claim that the vacuum has
no memory. It means that, in the stable lepton sector, responsiveness and
constraint history cancel into the fixed operating point. Non-equilibrium
departures from that cancellation are deliberately reserved for the later
Mujjabi Vacuum Memory Law and the tests in Paper~XII.

In this sense, the chirality phase is a memory-compatible phase: it is stable
because the local constraint history has settled, not because the medium has no
history.

\section{Setup: Three Equations, One Unknown}
\label{sec:setup}

We collect the three equations from Papers~II--III that govern $\theta$ and
$\phi_c$.

\paragraph{Chirality equilibrium (Paper~III, Theorem~2).}
\begin{equation}
  3\theta + \phi_c \;=\; \pi\,.
  \label{eq:equil}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Brannen mass parametrisation (Paper~II).}
\begin{equation}
  m_k \;=\; M\Bigl(1 + \sqrt{2}\cos\!\bigl(\theta + \tfrac{2\pi k}{3}\bigr)\Bigr)^2,
  \quad k = 0, 1, 2\,.
  \label{eq:brannen}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Koide invariant (Paper~II, Theorem~1).}
\begin{equation}
  Q \;\equiv\; \frac{\sum_k m_k}{(\sum_k \sqrt{m_k})^2}
  \;=\; \frac{2}{3}
  \quad \text{for all } M > 0 \text{ and all } \theta\,.
  \label{eq:koide}
\end{equation}

Equations~\eqref{eq:equil}--\eqref{eq:koide} have two unknowns: $\theta$ and
$\phi_c$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:equil} is one equation in two unknowns.
An additional condition is required.

\section{The $\mathbb{Z}_3$ Self-Consistency Condition}
\label{sec:self_consistency}

\subsection{The argument}

The Brannen parametrisation~\eqref{eq:brannen} is built on $\mathbb{Z}_3$:
the three modes are related by shifts of $2\pi/3$.
The $\mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry has a unique invariant: the Koide ratio
$Q = 2/3$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:koide}).

The chirality equilibrium~\eqref{eq:equil} breaks this $\mathbb{Z}_3$
symmetry: $\phi_c \neq 0$ shifts the equilibrium $\theta$ away from the
$\mathbb{Z}_3$-symmetric point $\theta = 0$ (where all three amplitudes
are equal).
The breaking is measured by the angle $3\theta$ (which appears in the
$\cos(3\theta + \phi_c)$ interaction term).

\textbf{Self-consistency condition:} the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ breaking angle
$3\theta$ must equal the unique $\mathbb{Z}_3$ invariant $Q$,
expressed as a dimensionless number in radians:
\begin{equation}
  3\theta \;=\; Q \;=\; \frac{2}{3}\,.
  \label{eq:self_consistency}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[$\phi_c$ from self-consistency]
  \label{thm:phi_c}
  The unique vacuum chirality phase consistent with the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
  structure of the trefoil is
  \begin{equation}
    \phi_c \;=\; \pi - \frac{2}{3}\,, \qquad \theta \;=\; \frac{2}{9}~\text{rad}\,.
    \label{eq:phi_c_result}
  \end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{outputs/paper_V/fig5_1_panel.pdf}
  \caption{The trefoil phase angle $\theta$. The value $\theta = 2/9$ rad derived from purely algebraic symmetry conditions exactly matches the numerical value fitted to PDG lepton masses in Paper~II.}
  \label{fig:theta_derived}
\end{figure}

\begin{proof}
  From Eq.~\eqref{eq:equil}: $\phi_c = \pi - 3\theta$.
  Substituting the self-consistency condition~\eqref{eq:self_consistency}
  ($3\theta = Q = 2/3$):
  \[
    \phi_c = \pi - \frac{2}{3}, \qquad \theta = \frac{2/3}{3} = \frac{2}{9}~\text{rad}\,.
  \]
  The uniqueness follows because: (a) $Q = 2/3$ is the unique Koide invariant
  of $\mathbb{Z}_3$ (Paper~II); (b) the equilibrium~\eqref{eq:equil}
  has a unique solution in the physical domain $\theta \in (0, \pi/3)$
  (Paper~III, Theorem~2).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Why $3\theta = Q$?}

The condition $3\theta = Q$ says: the angle of $\mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry
breaking (in the angular phase space of the trefoil) equals the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
mass invariant (a dimensionless ratio).
Both live in $[0, 1]$ in natural units, and both are set by the same
$\mathbb{Z}_3$ structure.

More precisely: the Brannen interaction energy $\lambda_c \cos(3\theta + \phi_c)$
breaks $\mathbb{Z}_3$.
The magnitude of the breaking is $3\theta$ (distance from the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
fixed point $\theta = 0$).
The only dimensionless number the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ structure can select as a
``natural'' breaking scale is $Q = 2/3$ --- the ratio that the symmetry
forces on all mass triplets.
The vacuum, in equilibrium, selects the breaking scale that matches its own
structural invariant.

\section{The Lepton Mass Prediction}
\label{sec:masses}

\subsection{The full chain}

With $\theta = 2/9$ rad, the Brannen amplitudes are:
\begin{equation}
  A_k \;=\; 1 + \sqrt{2}\cos\!\left(\frac{2}{9} + \frac{2\pi k}{3}\right),
  \quad k = 0, 1, 2\,.
  \label{eq:Ak}
\end{equation}
The electron corresponds to the mode with the smallest $A_k$:
\begin{equation}
  A_e \;=\; \min_k A_k \;=\; 0.040350\,.
  \label{eq:Ae}
\end{equation}
Setting $m_e = M A_e^2$ gives $M = m_e / A_e^2 = 313.86$~MeV.
The three masses are $m_k = M A_k^2$ (sorted ascending).

\paragraph{Irreducible inputs.}
The only input is $m_e$, which sets the unit of mass.
This is irreducible: no dimensionless theory can predict the absolute value
of a dimensionful quantity.
Given $m_e$, the remaining quantities in this construction follow inside the
model.

\paragraph{The three numbers that fix everything.}
The lepton sector is determined by three numbers:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $\alpha = 1/137.036$: fixes the geometry ($a = e^2/(m_e c^2)$, $R = \hbar/(m_e c)$).
  \item $Q = 2/3$: fixes the angular structure ($\theta = 2/9$, $\phi_c = \pi - 2/3$).
  \item $m_e$: fixes the mass scale ($M = m_e/A_e^2$, $\rho_0 = M/(c^2 a^3 g)$).
\end{enumerate}
All three are derived from CDFT (Papers~I, II, and the definition of the mass unit
respectively).

\section{The Significance of $3\theta = Q$}

The identity $3\theta = Q = 2/3$ is the key equation of this paper.
In plain language:

\begin{quote}
  \emph{The angle by which the vacuum chirality breaks the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
  symmetry of the trefoil --- measured in the angular phase space of the
  Brannen parametrisation --- equals the Koide ratio itself.}
\end{quote}

The Koide ratio $Q = 2/3$ is a pure number, forced by $\mathbb{Z}_3$
symmetry.
It appears as both:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a \emph{mass ratio}: $Q = (\sum m_k)/(\sum \sqrt{m_k})^2 = 2/3$, and
  \item an \emph{angular phase}: $3\theta = 2/3$~rad, the chirality breaking angle.
\end{itemize}

The vacuum ``selects'' the chirality phase $\phi_c = \pi - Q$ such that
the chirality breaking is commensurate with the symmetry it breaks.
This is the CDFT analogue of a Nambu--Goldstone condition: the vacuum
expectation value aligns with the symmetry invariant of the broken phase.

\section{Open Problems}

Within the assumptions of the model, the lepton-sector construction is complete.
The remaining open problems for future papers concern the extension beyond leptons:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$T(2,5)$ cinquefoil family.}
  Paper~IV predicted $M_5 = 460.4$~MeV.
  The cinquefoil has $\mathbb{Z}_5$ symmetry and five modes.
  The analogue of the present paper would derive $5\theta_5 = Q_5$
  (where $Q_5$ is the $\mathbb{Z}_5$ mass invariant).
  If the same self-consistency principle applies, $\theta_5$ is
  fixed by the $\mathbb{Z}_5$ Koide-like invariant of the cinquefoil.

\item \textbf{Why $Q = 2/3$ specifically?}
  The $\mathbb{Z}_3$ invariant $Q = 2/3$ was proved for the Brannen
  form in Paper~II.
  A deeper question: why is the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ Koide ratio $2/3$
  and not some other value?
  This may follow from the Brannen amplitudes $A_k = 1 + \sqrt{2}\cos(\cdot)$,
  where the coefficient $\sqrt{2}$ has a geometric origin in the trefoil
  (it is the ratio of the lobe amplitude to the background).

\item \textbf{Quark sector.}
  The CDFT torus knot hierarchy (Paper~III) predicts that knots $T(2,5)$
  and above correspond to further particle families.
  Whether the cinquefoil modes are quark-like (confined) or lepton-like
  (free) depends on the topology of the knot's self-intersection structure.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

The vacuum chirality phase $\phi_c$ is not an independent property of
the vacuum medium.
It is fixed by the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ self-consistency condition:
the chirality breaking angle $3\theta$ must equal the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
mass invariant $Q = 2/3$.
This gives $\phi_c = \pi - 2/3$ and $\theta = 2/9$~rad.

With one input ($m_e$ as the mass unit), CDFT predicts:
\begin{align*}
  m_\mu  &= 105.659~\text{MeV} \quad (+9.8~\text{ppm}),  \\
  m_\tau &= 1776.985~\text{MeV} \quad (+1.04\,\sigma),
\end{align*}
with the Koide formula exact.

Within the assumptions of the model, the CDFT lepton sector is internally
closed. The derivation chain from vacuum geometry to lepton masses requires
one irreducible external input (the unit of mass $m_e$) and zero adjustable
parameters.
