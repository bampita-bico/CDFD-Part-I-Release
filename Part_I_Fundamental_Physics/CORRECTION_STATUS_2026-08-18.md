# Part I Correction Status - 2026-08-18

This notice is the current correction record for the twelve-paper Part I
physics archive. It applies to the active release sources and PDFs in this
directory. It does not rewrite historical deposited records.

Read this notice before citing, distributing, or using any Part I paper as a
premise for further work.

## Binding Reading Rule

- `Phi`, `C`, `S`, `M_s`, and `Psi_s` are CDFT bookkeeping notation and
  candidate modelling variables. They are not independently derived laws of
  physics.
- Internal algebraic statements hold only under their stated parametrisation
  and axioms. They do not establish a physical vortex model without an
  independent physical mapping and empirical discriminant.
- Supplementary calculations reproduce the release-local model; they do not
  validate the physical hypotheses against observation.
- Parts II and III must not cite Part I as evidence that a universal
  flow-constraint law has been established.

## Claims Withdrawn or Demoted

The following claims must not be presented as established results of this
release:

- The kappa/fine-structure recovery is not an independent derivation. The
  vacuum-density route cancels to `kappa = 1/a`, and the published target uses
  the known fine-structure ratio.
- The `3 theta = Q` chirality closure and its downstream zero-parameter mass
  claims do not have an independent physical derivation.
- Trefoil selection is not established by the cited knot/soliton literature.
- Higher-family mass assignments, including Z5/M5 claims, are hypotheses or
  failed extrapolations, not observed-particle predictions.
- `Psi_s = Phi/C` is a postulated notational relation in this programme, not a
  physical theorem or an independent derivation.
- The collective-matter mappings add no demonstrated predictive value beyond
  field-native variables and models.
- Cosmology, dark-sector, black-hole, vacuum-memory, and engineering claims
  remain hypotheses only until an independent residual survives standard QED,
  GR, and domain-specific explanations.

available constrained-optimizer runs did not achieve a dense-grid-certified
finite-thickness equilibrium. Their raw lengths are diagnostic output, not
